# Executive Summary: Deutschlandticket Commuting Analysis — J&J Medical GmbH, Norderstedt

## What this is
A scenario analysis estimating how attractive public transport would be for a **synthetic population of 500 employees** living around Hamburg and Norderstedt, and how much Deutschlandticket adoption potential that population represents. Because real employee addresses can't be used, all employee locations and characteristics are synthetic — plausible, but not real.

## Headline results (this synthetic scenario, under the documented assumptions)

| Metric | Result |
|---|---|
| Within 30 minutes | ~2% |
| Within 45 minutes | ~18% |
| Within 60 minutes | ~40% |
| Over 60 minutes | ~60% |
| High adoption potential | ~13% |
| Medium adoption potential | ~32% |
| Low adoption potential | ~54% |

These are **estimated, not routed or observed** figures — see the methodology and limitations docs before treating any number here as precise.

## Strongest areas
Norderstedt itself and the Langenhorn corridor (served by the U1 line) show the shortest commutes and highest adoption-potential scores in this scenario.

## Weakest areas
Hamburg-Innenstadt and the AKN-only towns further out (Kaltenkirchen, Henstedt-Ulzburg) show the longest estimated commutes and lowest adoption potential — mainly a function of distance from the Norderstedt corridor and, for AKN-served areas, an added interchange penalty.

## Key factors influencing adoption potential
1. Estimated door-to-door commute time (heaviest weight)
2. Station connectivity — walking distance and number of transfers
3. Whether the employee already has easy car access
4. Current commuting mode

## Recommendations
- Target Deutschlandticket communication first toward Norderstedt/U1-corridor residents, where the convenience case is strongest.
- For AKN-only areas, lead with cost/sustainability messaging rather than convenience, since the time case is weaker.
- Employees without car access are a natural early-adopter segment across all areas.

## What this analysis cannot tell you
Actual price sensitivity, real willingness to pay, real HVV schedule reliability, or true employee behaviour — none of that exists in this synthetic scenario. Treat the percentages above as **indicative, within this synthetic scenario and based on the assumptions described**, not as a measurement of J&J's real workforce.
